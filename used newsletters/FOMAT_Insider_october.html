Across our research network, 11 study-site assignments are progressing through different stages: six in PSV, two in Awarded, and three at Greenlight. Some protocols are represented across multiple sites or Principal Investigators (PIs), highlighting the continued expansion of our research network.

Our network expands into North Carolina and endocrinology

Creedmoor Centre Endocrinology photo

Creedmoor Centre Endocrinology has joined FOMAT’s network, marking two important milestones: our first location in North Carolina and our entry into endocrinology. Led by Dr. Julia Warren-Ulanch, the practice expands our expertise in diabetes, thyroid disorders, and other endocrine conditions while helping grow our network beyond 40 sites nationwide.

Cardiology Associates of Michigan joins FOMAT’s network

Cardiology Associates of Michigan photo

FOMAT is partnering with Cardiology Associates of Michigan, a cardiology practice in Shelby Township. The group’s principal investigator has extensive research experience, and the practice plans to bring a research program into its private practice with FOMAT’s support.

What is Cardiology Associates of Michigan?

Cardiology Associates of Michigan is a cardiology practice serving Macomb and St. Clair counties, with locations in Shelby Township, Roseville, and East China. Over the past 25 years it has grown from a solo practice into a multi-clinician group that includes interventional and non-interventional cardiologists and advanced practice providers. Clinical research and patient education are among its core focus areas.

Metro Detroit Endocrinology Center joins FOMAT’s network

Metro Detroit Endocrinology Center photo

Building on our recent expansion into endocrinology, FOMAT welcomes Metro Detroit Endocrinology Center to our growing research network. With locations in Dearborn and Southfield, Michigan, the practice provides specialized care for adults and children with endocrine conditions, including diabetes, thyroid disorders, obesity, and adrenal and pituitary disorders. This new alliance further strengthens our capabilities in endocrinology and expands opportunities for future clinical research.

About Metro Detroit Endocrinology Center

Metro Detroit Endocrinology Center is an endocrinology practice in Dearborn, Michigan. It cares for adults and children with endocrine disorders, including diabetes, thyroid disease, obesity, and adrenal and pituitary conditions. Services include diabetes care, insulin pump management, hormonal testing, and thyroid ultrasound. The team evaluates each patient’s overall health and keeps primary care providers informed throughout treatment.
